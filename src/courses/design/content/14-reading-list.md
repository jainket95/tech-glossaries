> Books, not blog posts — design has an unusually strong canon of still-correct, decades-old books, which is a more durable starting point than archived articles with rotting URLs. Where an org is named instead of an article, check their current site.

## Foundations and usability

| Book | Why it's here |
|---|---|
| **The Design of Everyday Things** — Don Norman | Source of affordances/signifiers/the Norman door (ch. 8). About designed objects generally, which is exactly why it transfers to interfaces. |
| **Don't Make Me Think** — Steve Krug | The shortest, most practical usability book. Extends ch. 10's heuristics into a field guide. |
| **100 Things Every Designer Needs to Know About People** — Susan Weinschenk | A compact tour through the psychology ch. 5 only had room to sample, each backed by real research. |

## Visual design, typography, systems

| Book | Why it's here |
|---|---|
| **Refactoring UI** — Adam Wathan, Steve Schoger | Written for developers with no design training — closest thing to a visual sequel to chapters 2-4. |
| **Design Systems** — Alla Kholmatova | The most thorough treatment of chapters 6, 7, and 13's territory. |
| **Atomic Design** — Brad Frost (free at atomicdesign.bradfrost.com) | The full original atoms/molecules/organisms hierarchy from ch. 7. |

## Interaction, motion, content

| Book | Why it's here |
|---|---|
| **About Face** — Cooper, Reimann, Cronin | Dense, comprehensive interaction-design reference — heavier reading, worth it for edge cases ch. 8 couldn't cover. |
| **Microinteractions** — Dan Saffer | Full expansion of the trigger/rules/feedback/loops framework from ch. 8. |

## Organizations worth following directly

- **Nielsen Norman Group (nngroup.com)** — research behind ch. 10's heuristics and much of the empirical research cited across this course.
- **web.dev and MDN's accessibility docs** — the most reliable, actively maintained reference for ch. 11's implementation side.
- **A List Apart's archive** — older, less active now, but still one of the best sources on content strategy × IA (ch. 9).

## Tools worth understanding, not just using

**Figma** (Dev Mode from ch. 12, variables from ch. 6) · **Storybook** (ch. 7, 13) · **Style Dictionary** (ch. 6) · **axe DevTools** (ch. 11)
